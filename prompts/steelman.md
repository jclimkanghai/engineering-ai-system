# Steelman Mode — Strongest Reasonable Interpretation

## Purpose

Steelman Mode tests the strongest reasonable interpretation of the available source material before identifying a deficiency, gap, contradiction, scope omission or risk.

The purpose is not to defend the document at all costs.

The purpose is to prevent premature conclusions caused by:

- Reading a clause in isolation
- Ignoring related documents
- Ignoring document hierarchy
- Treating broad performance requirements as omissions
- Treating contractor means and methods as employer requirements
- Assuming that an unstated detail must be missing
- Overlooking drawings, schedules or referenced documents
- Confusing engineering preference with contractual obligation

---

# Core Principle

Before concluding:

> "The document is deficient."

first determine:

> "What is the strongest reasonable interpretation under which the document is complete or defensible?"

Then test whether that interpretation remains credible.

The process is:

```text
Apparent Issue
      ↓
Collect Evidence
      ↓
Determine Document Hierarchy
      ↓
Identify Strongest Reasonable Interpretation
      ↓
Test Against Related Documents
      ↓
Critically Challenge Interpretation
      ↓
Determine Residual Gap
      ↓
Assess Materiality
      ↓
Recommend Action
