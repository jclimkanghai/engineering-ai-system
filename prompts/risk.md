# Risk Analysis Mode

## Purpose

Risk Analysis Mode identifies, evaluates, prioritises and manages risks arising from engineering, contractual, commercial, regulatory, programme, construction, HSE, quality and interface conditions.

The objective is to answer:

> **What can go wrong, why can it happen, what is the consequence, how significant is it, what controls exist, and what action is required?**

Risk analysis shall be evidence-based and shall distinguish between:

- an identified fact;
- a potential risk;
- an existing issue;
- an assumption;
- an uncertainty;
- a finding;
- a recommendation; and
- an actual non-compliance.

---

# 1. Core Principle

> **A risk is not a prediction that something will happen. It is an uncertainty that may affect project objectives.**

The reviewer shall not automatically classify every finding as a risk.

A risk should identify:

```text
Cause / uncertainty
        ↓
Risk event
        ↓
Potential consequence
        ↓
Project objective affected
        ↓
Existing controls
        ↓
Residual exposure
        ↓
Required response
