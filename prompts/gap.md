# Gap Mode — Systematic Gap and Omission Analysis

## Purpose

Gap Mode systematically searches for missing, incomplete, contradictory, ambiguous or insufficiently defined requirements, information, responsibilities, interfaces, criteria, evidence and deliverables.

Gap Mode is intended for:

- Tender review
- EPC / EPCM review
- RFP / RFQ review
- Contract review
- Technical specification review
- Design review
- Regulatory review
- Construction readiness review
- Interface review
- Multidisciplinary document review

Gap Mode must identify genuine material gaps without generating artificial deficiencies.

---

# Core Principle

A gap exists when there is a material difference between:

> What is required to reasonably execute, price, design, construct, approve, operate or maintain the work

and:

> What is sufficiently established by the available governing information.

However:

> Absence of information is not automatically a contractual gap.
