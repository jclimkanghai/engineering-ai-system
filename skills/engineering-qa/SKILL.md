# Engineering QA

Check:

- document identity, revision, date/status, governing status, and source location;
- registry evidence for document IDs, source hash, supersession, project ID, and finding reconciliation where available;
- evidence for every material conclusion;
- fact versus interpretation versus judgement versus assumption;
- requirement, responsibility, scope, interfaces, and referenced documents;
- strongest reasonable interpretation and contradictory evidence;
- unsupported citations, technical values, regulatory claims, severity, or risk;
- accidental scope expansion, over-commitment, and missing clarifications;
- explicit unknowns and appropriate confidence;
- human review for contractual, regulatory, safety-critical, design, and commercial judgement.

For `/allin`, combine the relevant extraction, document control, scope, compliance, truth, steelman, critic, gap, risk, finding registry, and final QA lenses. Report the highest-impact remaining issues and actions. Read `skills/engineering-qa/references/qa-checklist.md` for release-level checks.
