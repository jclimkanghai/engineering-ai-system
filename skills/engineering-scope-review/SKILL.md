# Engineering Scope Review

For each scope item:

1. Identify the source requirement and location.
2. Verify document identity, revision, status, governing hierarchy, and supersession where registry data is available.
3. Identify the named responsible party.
4. Determine mandatory, advisory, or informational status.
5. Check related documents, boundaries, interfaces, dependencies, exclusions, addenda, clarifications, and later revisions.
6. Test the strongest reasonable interpretation.
7. Classify as `IN_SCOPE`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `THIRD_PARTY`, `CLIENT_INPUT`, `INTERFACE`, `OPTIONAL`, `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED`, or `UNKNOWN`.
8. Record technical, commercial, programme, and responsibility implications.

Do not classify an item as `OUT_OF_SCOPE` solely because it is not mentioned. Do not classify it as `IN_SCOPE` merely because it is common practice. Use `prompts/scope.md`, `prompts/contractual.md`, and `skills/engineering-scope-review/references/scope-method.md` for detailed analysis.
